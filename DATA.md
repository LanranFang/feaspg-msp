# Data and provenance

## What is included

The controlled benchmark is synthetic. Its configurations and seeds are in
`inputs/bundles.json`; data are generated by
`efficiency_switching_hydro/data.py`. Smaller training sets are prefixes of
the original full pool. Generating a smaller pool directly from the same seed
would change the random-number sequence and is deliberately avoided.

For ONS and CAMELS-BR, `real_data_hydro_experiments/data/processed/` contains:

- The numerical reservoir system and objective configuration.
- The chronological fitting block and fitted periodic autoregressive model.
- Source/protocol provenance and selected-series metadata.
- Small locked historical evaluation arrays and their original hashes.

ONS uses four equivalent-energy aggregates. CAMELS-BR uses twelve non-nested
catchments with semi-synthetic reservoirs; this is not a verified physical dam
network. Model-generated test paths and historical held-out paths have distinct
roles and are not interchangeable.

`artifact/real_inputs.py` samples the fitted models using the original seeds.
The 5,000-path A1 fitting pool concatenates a 2,500-path pool at seed 61101 with
a separately generated 2,500-path extension at seed 61606. Validation uses
seed 61202 and 500 paths; testing uses seed 61303 and 4,096 paths. The
historical replay is loaded separately. Every reconstructed array is checked
against a fingerprint exported from the original experiment, including
hydrology state, regimes and source-path identifiers.

## Upstream sources and preprocessing

The ONS source is the official Brazilian National System Operator's natural
energy, load, and associated system records. Source filenames, time splits,
aggregation rules and reconstruction choices are recorded in
`data/processed/ons_subsystems/provenance.json` and the associated audit files
under `real_data_hydro_experiments/`.

CAMELS-BR attribution follows Chagas et al. (2020), with the versioned input
record used by the experiment pinned in
`real_hydro/data/camels_br.py` (Zenodo record 15025488). Selection, missing-data
handling and reservoir construction are implemented in
`real_hydro/data/camels_br.py` and `real_hydro/benchmarks/camels_br.py`.

The upstream raw archives are not bundled. The package is sufficient for
training/evaluating all submitted benchmark models and recreating their
model-generated inputs without network access. Rebuilding every raw-data
cleaning step additionally requires those original source archives. The
legacy `real_hydro.cli audit` / `prepare` commands validate those raw archives
as well as the prepared bundle and therefore are not the offline artifact
verification commands. Use `python -m artifact.inputs --verify-only` and
`python -m artifact.verify_manifest` for this package.

The package does not include third-party library implementations, solver
licenses, access credentials, or the large downloaded raw-data archives.
The respective upstream terms apply to the source data and installed
dependencies.
